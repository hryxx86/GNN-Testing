## 1. Six-slice synthesis

### 1.1 GNN / graph-based stock prediction

State of play: the field has implicitly conceded that static predefined graphs
do not work — every 2026 contribution is a learned/dynamic/purified graph
(GAPNet plug-in learned graphs arXiv:2602.00888; soft-threshold sparsification
STN-TGAT arXiv:2607.19385; SSM-modeled graph evolution S³G ICASSP'26
arXiv:2603.24236; "structural purification" to remove relational noise ACT
arXiv:2604.20204). Hypergraphs are the default relational object (MaGNet,
H3M-SSMoEs, 2× ICAIF'25). LLM×graph is the fastest-growing strand (Relational
Probing, ICLR'26 workshop arXiv:2604.10212: relation head over LLM hidden
states; TRACE arXiv:2603.12500: LLM-grounded KG evidence chains) and has the
weakest evaluation (proprietary data, ~55% accuracy with no chance test).

Rigor is stagnant: of the 10 core in-window papers, 8 use a single
chronological split, 2 report seeds, 1 runs any named significance test, 0
correct for multiple testing; the 2013–2017 NASDAQ/NYSE dataset is still in
active use. Reported ICs on identical universes are mutually incompatible
(CSI300: 0.069 in ACT vs 0.141–0.154 in USTGCN, MDPI Electronics 15(6):1317).

Two findings by *proponents* support our thesis: (a) Wade arXiv:2605.19278
(S&P 500 GraphSAGE, vol→portfolio): best-MSE, best-rank-IC and best-Sharpe are
three different models; correlation-graph density explodes in crises
(9.2%→93.3% of edges, COVID) causing oversmoothing. (b) ACT/STN-TGAT motivate
their architectures by the need to remove noise that raw graphs inject.

**No critique or replication paper of GNN stock prediction exists in the
window. Venue drift is visible: GNN-stock work has left AAAI/KDD/WWW main
tracks for ICASSP/ICAIF/workshops/MDPI/preprints.**

### 1.2 LLMs in finance

Memorization/look-ahead went from caveat to subfield: detection (Lookahead
Propensity, arXiv:2512.23847 — accuracy loads on memorization probability
in-cutoff, collapses post-cutoff), standardized benchmarking (Look-Ahead-Bench
arXiv:2601.13770), inference-time suppression (FinCAD arXiv:2605.24564, −67%
memorized in-sample returns, OOS unchanged), data-side masking + Barra-style
attribution (KTD-Fin arXiv:2605.28359: agent returns ≈ beta + style, little
alpha), and by-construction fixes (chronologically consistent LLMs —
ChronoBERT/ChronoGPT arXiv:2502.21206 — used by Zhang & G. Zhou SSRN 6712298
for earnings-call signals; live evaluation, Agent Market Arena).

The critical papers hold the prestige venues (FINSABER, KDD'26 oral: LLM agent
alpha dies under broad universes/long horizons; 5-bias position paper
arXiv:2602.14233, Lopez-Lira/Zohren et al.: ≤28% of 164 papers discuss even
one bias); the positive results are mostly unrefereed. The field is retreating
from "LLM as trader" to "LLM as deterministic factor/feature generator"
(AlphaForgeBench arXiv:2602.18481; AlphaBench ICLR'26). Nobody applies SPA/FDR
to LLM-signal families.

### 1.3 Foundation models / capacity

Convergent negative: zero-shot generic TSFMs (Chronos/TimesFM/Moirai) ≈ random
walk on first-moment returns, lose to GBM ensembles (Re(Visiting)
arXiv:2511.18578; Noguer i Alonso & Franklin arXiv:2606.27100 — DM rejects in
only 2 of asset×model cells; Brini arXiv:2607.05291 — vol transfers, returns
don't). Positive evidence lives in domain pretraining (Kronos, AAAI 2026,
arXiv:2508.02739: 12B K-lines, 45 exchanges, 4M–500M param family, open
source). The virtue-of-complexity debate is in working-paper trench warfare
(Nagel NBER w34104 "Seemingly Virtuous Complexity" vs Kelly-Malamud SSRN
5346842; Buncic implementation-artifact critique; synthesis arXiv:2604.17166:
capacity helps only as a search space for sparse priced structure). **No
controlled params×data×seeds sweep on cross-sectional ranking exists.** The
one cost-aware equity benchmark (arXiv:2606.09420, CRSP, 15 models, fixed
protocol) finds no architecture dominates and ALL models net-negative at
20 bps.

### 1.4 Evaluation rigor / meta-science

The 2026 rigor wave targets LLM agents almost exclusively (8+ papers);
classical supervised GNN/DL ranking received none of it. Leakage is now a
measured quantity: one-switch protocol ablation (arXiv:2605.23959 — notable:
future-informed graph structure is a *weak* inflation channel), engine
implementation risk (arXiv:2603.20319: up to 3.71% divergence across backtest
engines under costs), workflow-level falsification audits with effective-
multiplicity adjustment (Spurious Predictability, arXiv:2604.15531).

Direct answer obtained: **nobody in Jan–Jul 2026 combines (a) pre-registration,
(b) equal-budget model ladders, (c) SPA + FDR family control for model
comparison on equities.** Three partial exceptions only (HindsightBench
arXiv:2607.18867 pre-registers an LLM behavioral audit; arXiv:2512.12924
publishes an honest null p=0.34 under pre-specified walk-forward; 2604.15531
is post-hoc). Anytime-valid / e-value sequential testing is mature in
statistics (arXiv:2505.09090, 2606.00878) but has not crossed into fin-ML
strategy evaluation.

Seed variance: arXiv:2603.16886 (918 experiments, MSE forecasting, intraday
crypto/FX/equities) finds seed = 0.01% of variance and blesses 3-seed
protocols — in direct tension with our rank-IC world (GAT-21d 5-seed CV=55%,
see memory/`project_gat21d_lucky_seed`). No published seed-variance
decomposition exists for cross-sectional rank-IC pipelines.

### 1.5 Regime / non-stationarity

"Regime-gate/MoE" is the 2026 architectural consensus (ReCAP KDD'26
arXiv:2606.00143 policy library + gate; PRISM-VQ IJCAI'26 arXiv:2605.13407
VQ + structure-conditioned MoE), but **no paper performs inference on the
conditional claim itself** — no test of regime×model interaction, no
Giacomini-White conditional predictive ability tests anywhere in the window.
Honest conditional results exist at the margins: return predictability
concentrated in low-vol regimes after costs (arXiv:2606.09478); selective
adaptation gates fail on financial data (ADOWIP arXiv:2606.25068, declared
negative); static regime→model selection rules mostly fail (arXiv:2605.01608).
Chen & Welch arXiv:2607.06502 set the effect-size prior: ~200 published
anomalies → 7 bp/month post-2005 in non-micro caps. Finance-specific
invariant/causal representation learning stalled (no successor to
InvariantStock TMLR'24 in window).

### 1.6 Losses / decision-focused / design choices

Flagship anchor: Chen, Hanauer & Kalsbach (SSRN 5031755, FoFI 2026) — 1,056
models over 7 design choices; **non-standard error = 1.59× standard error**;
algorithm is NOT the dominant choice (post-publication treatment, training
window, target transformation matter as much or more); monthly TMB spread
0.13%–1.98% across designs. Related: label-horizon ≠ inference-horizon can be
optimal (arXiv:2602.03395); rank-IC-aligned losses consolidate into a subfield
(LambdaRankIC arXiv:2605.00501 — implemented on XGBoost, not deep; LTR asset
pricing SSRN 6348379; loss sweep at fixed Transformer arXiv:2510.14156).
Decision-focused learning enters self-criticism (SPO causes prediction
inflation + turnover blow-up, arXiv:2605.01176). Turnover is the diagnostic
where predictive metrics are silent: optimizer choice alone → ~3× turnover
dispersion at equal accuracy (MIT Poggio lab, arXiv:2603.02620); 3-seed
averaging removes most seed noise in E2E Sharpe training (arXiv:2607.00475);
50-seed/top-10 protocol codified (Oxford benchmark arXiv:2603.01820).

Honest nulls from the search itself: no 2026 deep→simple distillation paper
for equity ranking; no dedicated "undertuned baseline" study; no four-way
{architecture, loss, tuning, seed} decomposition.

## 2. Cross-cutting patterns

1. Rigor is the scarce resource everywhere; critique papers hold top venues,
   positive papers drift to weak venues. Our protocol (prereg + equal-budget +
   SPA/FDR + 10 seeds + costs) remains ahead of every 2026 paper surveyed.
2. The field is converging on OUR framing from multiple directions —
   component attribution over model worship (agent-vs-backbone, plug-in graph
   deltas, one-switch ablations, design-choice nonstandard errors) — but
   without inference machinery.
3. Conditionality is the zeitgeist (regime gates, MoE, "when does X help")
   yet nobody tests the conditional claim with valid inference.
4. Architecture-innovation returns are visibly diminishing: head-only tweak
   ΔIC ≈ +0.007 (RankGLU) ≈ typical claimed "graph gains"; no-winner
   benchmarks; net-negative at 20 bps.
5. Memorization-safe LLM tooling (Chrono* models, LAP) now makes LLM-feature
   research *defensible* for the first time.

## 4. Idea candidates (A–F)

**A. "Nonstandard Errors in Deep Cross-Sectional Ranking"** (G3+G4+G7+G8)
Variance decomposition of rank-IC / net-Sharpe / turnover over design axes
{graph on/off, architecture family, loss (incl. ranking losses), label
horizon, tuning budget, seed, universe, split protocol}, mixed-effects on a
fractional-factorial grid; large fraction of cells already computed (2160
confirmatory cells + 360 horizon-ablation + 150 arch-comparison + M14 trials
sweep + loss horse race). Anchor literature: nonstandard errors (Menkveld et
al.; Chen-Hanauer-Kalsbach). Absorbs a capacity axis (G7) and a distillation
arm (G8). Headline estimand: variance shares + design-choice nonstandard
error for deep ranking pipelines.

**B. "When, Exactly? Conditional Predictive Ability of Graph Components"** (G5)
Re-use paper-1 saved daily predictions; Giacomini-White conditional
predictive-ability tests on daily loss differentials (T≈749/universe) with a
pre-registered conditioning set (T-1 VIX, cross-sectional dispersion,
correlation-graph density, realized-vol regime, breadth); FDR over the
conditioning family. Tests the regime-gate literature's premise. Near-zero
compute (no retraining). Also produces ICLR-rebuttal ammunition ("is the
graph penalty regime-specific?" — answered with inference).

**C. LLM text signals under the confirmatory protocol** (G6): ChronoBERT/
chronologically-consistent embeddings of our existing news corpus → feature
arm + LLM-edge arm under the frozen two-family protocol, LAP-stratified.
**D. Registered-report living benchmark platform** (G2 productized).
**E. Standalone capacity/VoC study for the cross-section** (G7).
**F. Standalone distillation study** (G8).

### 4b. Second-batch candidates (added 2026-07-27, per H博士 request for more ideas)

**G. Registered live forward test.** Freeze paper-1 models; run true
forward inference from 2026-08 onward; pre-register the monitoring rule.
Pairs with G9: monitor via anytime-valid e-process (Choe–Ramdas-style
sequential forecast comparison) → first anytime-valid live evaluation in
fin-ML. Near-zero compute; value accrues with calendar time (start ASAP).
**H. The graph multiverse.** Specification-curve / multiverse analysis over
graph-construction researcher degrees of freedom (corr threshold × window ×
weighting × edge type) — directly resolves our own L9. Medium compute.
**I. Planted-signal power audits.** Generalize our positive control into a
methodology: inject graph-/text-/factor-borne signal at controlled strength
into real panels → empirical detection-threshold curves per architecture;
answers "is a null no-signal or no-power?" for any component study.
**J. Cross-market confirmatory replication (CSI300 home turf).** Port the
frozen two-family protocol to the dataset where GNN wins are claimed
(ACT/StockMamba/Qlib treadmill); scoped ladder (L0/L1/L2 + 1 edge arm).
Resolves L7; ~2 A100-days. Can absorb B (GW conditional tests per market).
**K. Sparse-subgraph hypothesis.** Test arXiv:2604.17166's "capacity as
search space for sparse structure" on edges: learned sparse masks over the
correlation graph under complementary-pairs stability selection (error-
controlled edge selection). Bridges our null with the learned-sparse trend.
**L. Evidence audit / meta-analysis of published GNN-stock results.**
Extract effect sizes + test statistics from ~30 published papers; quantify
reporting absence, excess significance, publication bias;半 already done in
lit_benchmark forensic matrix + this scan. Zero compute. Caveat: most papers
report no p-values → frame as evidence audit, not classic p-curve.
**M. The peer-momentum paradox (mechanism).** Linked-firm/industry momentum
is a documented anomaly (Moskowitz-Grinblatt; Cohen-Frazzini); GNNs should
harvest it but don't. Establish anomaly presence on our panel (portfolio
sorts, cheap), then adjudicate: decayed (Chen-Welch) vs present-but-missed
(representation/optimization failure, probed via planted-signal + probing).
Either branch publishable; answers the "why" paper 1 left open. Public-data
edges only (sector, correlation lead-lag, Wikidata) — no FactSet needed.
**N. Seed ensembles vs architecture innovation at equal compute.** Does a
10-seed MLP ensemble beat every single-seed GNN arm? Computable TODAY from
saved predictions. Likely a section inside A rather than standalone.

Quick verdicts (2026-07-27 debate pass): top new picks = M (mechanism,
most distinctive), L (zero compute, meta-science wave), G (time-critical,
start regardless), J (strongest L7 answer; natural paper-2 spine that can
absorb B). N folds into A; H folds into A as an axis or into J; I is the
methods insurance for any null-heavy follow-up; K is the riskiest/most
compute-hungry. PENDING H博士.
