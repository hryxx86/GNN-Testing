## 0. Why this exists

The paper's Limitation L1 says Universe C's positive results are "suggestive pending leak-free re-selection". The C5 run (docs/analysis.md 2026-09-11-a) could not discharge that promise because the 5-group selection behind C5 was itself scored on quarters inside the evaluation window (test-informed). The advisor's request is a *leak-free re-selection*. C-pre is the smallest experiment that answers it directly: re-select Universe C's feature basis using **only information dated before the tuning-validation window and the 12-fold evaluation window**, then re-tune and re-evaluate L0 (LightGBM) and L1 (MLP) under the frozen confirmatory protocol.

**What C-pre can claim**: an estimate of the MLP−LightGBM contrast (and per-arm IC) on a feature basis whose selection used pre-evaluation information only, with a paired daily contrast against C, B and C5.
**What C-pre cannot claim**: it does not identify how much selection leakage inflated C (column sets differ, both arms are re-tuned); it is not confirmatory (designed after the paper's test results were known; no pre-registered family; nominal, unadjusted p-values); it does not change any confirmatory table. Universe B remains the leak-free price-volume anchor.

**Scope (Codex TP1-A)**: C-pre re-evaluates only L0 and L1, so it addresses the feature-selection component of L1's qualification for the MLP−LightGBM contrast. Graph/edge contrasts in Universe C are not re-evaluated, the panel's documented non-point-in-time composition (paper L8) remains, and the analysis was designed after the test results were known. Paper-side description accepted at TP1-A: "We conducted a retrospective sensitivity analysis using feature re-selection whose scoring and grouping inputs were restricted to information through June 2022, conditional on the study's fixed stock universe. We re-tuned and evaluated LightGBM and MLP under the existing evaluation protocol." C-pre can supply the requested re-selection evidence for MLP vs LightGBM; it cannot turn C's original results into clean confirmatory evidence.

## 1. Question and pre-declared reading (frozen before any model run)

**Q**: On the C-pre basis, is L1−L0 (seed-averaged daily ΔIC, 12 folds × 10 seeds) of the same sign and comparable magnitude as C (+0.0148), B (+0.0143) and C5 (+0.0134)?

Pre-declared reporting (the same estimator, CI and HAC lags as C5; every quantity below is computed from C-pre's own results — nothing is inherited from C5/C/B; Codex TP1-A A-01/A-02):

- **Outcome branches are exhaustive** on the 21d-block-bootstrap 95% CI of Δ = L1−L0: (a) entirely above 0, (b) entirely below 0, (c) contains 0. Sentence for (a)/(b): "On a basis whose selection used pre-evaluation information only, the MLP−LightGBM contrast is Δ = … [CI]; nominal HLN p = … (NW auto lag) / … (lag 21); the interval lies entirely above/below zero." Sentence for (c): "The interval contains zero; this does not establish absence of a contrast." In every branch |Δ| is compared with **C-pre's own** approximate nominal MDE = 2.8 × its own block-bootstrap SE (the ≈0.020 of C5/C/B is a planning reference only); if the CI verdict and either HLN test disagree at 5%, the disagreement is reported as such and is not upgraded to a robust detection. No "confirms C", no "leak-free confirmation".
- **Paired daily contrasts** C − C-pre, B − C-pre, C5 − C-pre: reported as the absolute paired change with its CI, both HLN lags and its own MDE. **No proportional ("halved / doubled") inference** — the comparator is itself an estimate and a non-rejection is not equivalence. If a paired CI excludes 0 and is positive: "the comparator's contrast exceeds the C-pre contrast; consistent with, but not an identification of, selection inflation, because the column sets differ and both arms were re-tuned."
- **Always**: "C-pre does not estimate how much leakage inflated C; it provides the clean-basis estimate of the same contrast, where 'clean basis' means feature re-selection whose scoring and grouping inputs are bounded by 2022-06-30, conditional on the study's fixed stock panel and on a protocol chosen retrospectively. B remains the leak-free anchor." Fold-9 (2025Q2) share and ex-fold-9 stats, per-seed k/10 (LOSO m implied), the finalists' tuning-window val-IC (the HP-search / early-stopping metric — outside the feature-selection sample, not an independent validation), and parameter counts are disclosed as for C5.

Forbidden wording is the C5 list (docs/analysis.md 2026-09-11-a) plus "definitive check", "leak-free confirmation", and any halving/doubling statement.

## 3. Frozen selector

### 3.1 Selection window
`D_sel` = the feature dates in the tuning fold's **train** mask, `create_fold_masks(TUNE_FOLD, all_dates, HORIZON)` with `TUNE_FOLD = {'train_end': '2022-06-30', 'val_end': '2022-12-31'}` and `TRAIN_START = '2021-07-01'` → feature dates 2021-07-01 … 2022-05-31 whose 21-day label ends ≤ 2022-06-30 (231 dates per Codex TP1-B; the selector asserts the count and archives the first/last date). Strictly before the tuning validation window (2022H2) and the evaluation window (2023Q1–2025Q4). Group memberships (§3.4) were calibrated on 2021-01-29 … 2022-01-27, also pre-test (verified by Codex TP1-B).

### 3.2 Per-feature daily IC and eligibility
For feature f and date t ∈ D_sel: cross-section = stocks with a valid label and a finite feature value. Date t is **eligible** for f iff that cross-section has ≥ 30 stocks and the feature values are non-constant (std > 1e-9). IC_f(t) = Spearman(feature_f(t, ·), label(t, ·)) on the eligible cross-section. (These are the conditions used by `analyze_plan_aaa_t1_diagnostic.compute_mean_ic`; kept for continuity.)

### 3.3 Coverage rule (CODEX-B-01) — **minimum-coverage, feature-specific dates**
coverage(f) = |eligible dates of f| / |D_sel|. A feature is **scored** iff coverage(f) ≥ **τ = 0.50**; otherwise it is **UNSCORED** and contributes nothing (undefined IC is never treated as 0). Known consequence, stated before scoring: `hc_mom12m` (252-session look-back on a panel that starts 2021-01-29) is non-constant on 85/231 dates = 0.37 → UNSCORED; every other candidate is expected to clear τ (the selector archives every feature's count). Alternatives considered and rejected: common eligible dates (would shrink every feature to 85 days); extra pre-cutoff history (not present in the 5-year panel; a refetch is a new data build, out of scope).
Verified 2026-09-11 (Claude on the panel; independently by Codex TP1-A): all 167 other candidates are eligible on 231/231 dates; `hc_mom12m` on 85/231 (2022-01-28 … 2022-05-31). Consequently the τ = 0.75 ranking is identical to the frozen τ = 0.50 ranking on these artifacts, and τ = 0 differs only by admitting `hc_mom12m` scored on its 85 dates.
Selector-robustness report (rankings only, **no model runs**): the group ranking is also tabulated at τ = 0 (admit the short-history feature), with top-15 overlap vs the frozen τ = 0.50 ranking. It measures sensitivity to admitting that one feature, not general selector stability; the frozen rule stays τ = 0.50 regardless of that table.

### 3.4 Group score, ranking, tie-break
Groups = the 61 Plan-AAA groups, memberships from `artifacts/plan_aaa/groups_168.json` (reused, not re-clustered). For group g: **S(g) = mean over its scored members m of |IC̄_m|**, where IC̄_m = time-mean of IC_m(t) over m's eligible dates (i.e. mean across members of each member's absolute time-mean IC). A group with zero scored members is UNRANKED. Rank by S descending; ties by number of scored members descending, then group_id ascending (never by the test-ranked `ranking.csv` order). Archive per group: S, n_members, n_scored, member IC̄ and coverage.

### 3.5 Column set
**Top-15 groups by S** → C-pre columns = union of **all** members of those groups (group is the unit of selection, as in Plan AAA → C), ordered group by group (rank order) then member order from `groups_168.json`. An UNSCORED member of a selected group is still included (built as in C); with the τ above this cannot happen for hc_mom12m because its group is a singleton and therefore UNRANKED. Width is data-determined.

### 3.6 Archive (`artifacts/storya_cpre_select/`, git-whitelisted)
`feature_scores.csv` (feature, source, coverage, n_eligible, IC̄, |IC̄|, scored flag, exclusion reason), `eligible_dates.json` (per-feature eligible-date indices, not only counts), `group_scores.csv`, `selection.json` (rules and τ; window first/last date and count; the resolved ordered ticker axis (501) and date axis (count, first/last, md5 of the full lists); ordered column list + md5; input md5s: alpha158 npy/meta, phase5 npy, prices csv, sectors csv, news-events ticker membership (these define part_a's ticker axis), groups_168.json; git rev of the **committed** selector source (the selector is committed after TP2 and before it runs — Codex TP1-A A-04); timestamp), `selector_robustness.csv` (τ = 0 variant), `summary.md`. The tuning `execution` block and main12 `_run_provenance.json` record `selection.json`'s md5 so selection → tuning → evaluation are linked.

## 4. Downstream protocol — identical to C5

Universe id **`CPRE`**, `UNIVERSE_IDX['CPRE'] = 3` → cell_id block [3600, 4799] (disjoint from confirmatory [0, 2399] and C5 [2400, 3599]; startup assert extended per sensitivity block). Then, unchanged from the C5 pipeline: tune L0 and L1 (Optuna 30 trials, top-5 finalists × tune seeds [11, 22, 33], train ≤ 2022-06-30 / val 2022H2, frozen correlation snapshot ≤ train_end) → `frozen_hparams_cpre.json` (subset merge, md5 archived with the two tune JSONs and sqlite studies) → 240 cells (12 expanding folds 2023Q1–2025Q4, 21d purge, 10 canonical seeds, per-fold train-only winsorize/standardize) → `compute_family1_ladder.py --universes CPRE --arms L0,L1 --sensitivity` (raw HLN p at NW auto lag and lag 21; 21d stationary block bootstrap 5000, seed 86; MDE ≈ 2.8×SE; no BH/SPA/L7) → sensitivity analysis (integrity gate incl. per-cell calendar == frozen calendar and feature_names == `UNIVERSE_CPRE_NAMES`; k/10; LOSO; ex-fold-9; paired daily contrasts C − CPRE, B − CPRE, C5 − CPRE on seed-averaged series with per-fold calendar alignment; hparam and parameter-count report; tests-reported inventory). Frozen HP injection gate and provenance (`_run_provenance.json`, tune `execution` block) as in C5, plus the selection.json md5.

Note on the selector (Codex TP1-A A-03): it uses neither evaluated model's fitted importance nor any evaluation-period performance (unlike Plan AAA's NN permutation importance scored on test quarters). It does select marginally predictive features and thereby fixes the representation on which both model classes compete, so results are conditional on this common selection rule; **no architecture-neutrality guarantee is claimed**. The tuning validation window (2022H2) is not used by the selector; the finalists' val-IC is therefore outside the feature-selection sample (it was not for C), while remaining the HP-search / early-stopping metric rather than an independent validation.
