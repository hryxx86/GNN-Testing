# Plan AAA: 168-feature Grouped Permutation Δ-IC Ranking

## §1. Context

### 1.1 Background

- Plan Z++ Part A (2026-04-19) constructed S1-S5 subsets via grouped permutation Δ-IC ranking *within a hand-curated 10-feature universe* (ret_mean_{5,10,21}d, ret_std_{5,10,21}d, mom12m, maxret, dolvol, CORR5).
- The 10 features were sourced from Phase 5 baseline + classical alpha factor literature (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993, Bali et al. 2011, Amihud 2002), not data-driven from Alpha158.
- Hansen SPA aggregate-level comparison (S1 vs S8) showed no significant difference, but this is aggregate, not feature-level.

### 1.2 The Limitation Codex + H博士 Exposed

- 158 dimensions in Alpha158 were never ranked individually by our production method.
- Option B (2026-05-22, LightGBM gain importance on Alpha158) found 4/10 hand-curated concepts in Alpha158 top-30 by gain; 3 outside top-100 (CORR5 rank 114, dolvol rank 118, ret_mean_5d rank 136).
- But Option B uses LightGBM GBDT, not our SAGE-Mean/MLP GNN — methodologically inconsistent with our production setup. LightGBM gain ≠ IC; GBDT non-linearities ≠ GNN message-passing.

### 1.3 Why Plan AAA

- Plan AAA extends Plan Z++ Part A's *exact* methodology to a 168-feature universe (158 Alpha158 + 10 hand-curated).
- Uses production models (SAGE-Mean + MLP), production metric (IC), and production permutation protocol — no methodological drift.
- Result is methodologically consistent answer to "what would Plan Z++ Part A have said if universe had been 168 instead of 10?"

### 1.4 Pre-commitment to Avoid p-Hacking

**Result will be reported in paper v3 §7 regardless of direction.** Three possible outcomes + paper response committed in advance:

| Outcome | Paper v3 response |
|---|---|
| (a) Hand-curated 10 groups all rank in 168-universe top-K | Universe choice validated; strengthens parsimony argument |
| (b) Some hand-curated groups rank low | Disclose honestly; report which concepts under-perform in extended universe |
| (c) All hand-curated groups rank low | Universe choice flagged as suboptimal; results conditional; recommend Plan AAA-derived universe for future research |

## §2. Universe Scope (KEY DECISION REQUIRING REVIEW)

### 2.1 Composition

**Total: 168 features = 158 Alpha158 + 10 hand-curated**

Hand-curated 10 (Phase 5 + classical alpha factors):
1. `hc_ret_mean_5d` (5-day daily return mean)
2. `hc_ret_mean_10d`
3. `hc_ret_mean_21d`
4. `hc_ret_std_5d` (5-day daily return std)
5. `hc_ret_std_10d`
6. `hc_ret_std_21d`
7. `hc_mom12m` (12-month momentum)
8. `hc_maxret` (max 5-day return)
9. `hc_dolvol` (dollar volume)
10. `hc_CORR5` (5-day sector correlation)

Alpha158 158 (qlib.contrib.data.loader.Alpha158DL default config; sample: KMID, KLEN, ROC5, ROC10, ROC20, STD5, STD10, STD20, MAX5, MAX10, CORR5, CORR10, CORR20, CORR60, BETA5, BETA60, etc.)

### 2.2 CORR5 Overlap Handling

- `hc_CORR5` and Alpha158's `CORR5` are mathematically identical (5-day sector correlation, same formula).
- **Design choice (REVIEW REQUIRED)**: Keep both. Clustering at |ρ|>0.6 will automatically merge them into one group; group label will be assigned by Part A's complete-link algorithm.
- Rationale: Removing duplicate preemptively introduces a design choice; letting clustering handle it preserves Part A protocol.

### 2.3 Conceptually-Similar but Mathematically-Distinct Pairs

| Hand-curated | Alpha158 semantic equiv | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| hc_ret_mean_5d | ROC5 | Different: ret_mean = avg(daily_ret), ROC5 = close/close[-5] - 1 |
| hc_ret_std_5d | STD5 | Different: hc = std of daily returns; Alpha158 STD = std of closes |
| hc_mom12m | ROC60 | Different: hc = 252-day momentum; Alpha158 ROC60 = 60-day |
| hc_maxret | MAX5 / MAX10 | Different: hc uses max(daily_ret); Alpha158 uses MAX over price level |

These pairs are highly correlated (likely |ρ|=0.6-0.9) and will be grouped by clustering. They are **kept separate** in the universe to let clustering surface the redundancy.

### 2.4 Pre-processing

- All 168 features loaded from raw sources (NOT pre-winsorized at build time, per 2026-05-22-a lesson):
  - Hand-curated 10: computed on-the-fly from `data/reference/sp500_5y_prices.csv` (`part_a.load_data_and_features()` provides this)
  - Alpha158 158: `data/reference/sp500_5y_alpha158_features_raw.npy` (raw, pre-winsor)
- Concatenate to (T, N, 168) tensor.
- Per-fold train-only winsorization at p1/p99 (Plan Z++ Phase 0 protocol).
- Per-fold scaler (mean/std fit on train days only).

## §3. Methodology (Verbatim Inheritance from Plan Z++ Part A)

### 3.1 Step 1: Feature Grouping (preregistration artifact)

- Compute Spearman correlation matrix on fold-0 train slice (after winsor + scaler, 168×168 matrix).
- Complete-link hierarchical clustering at |ρ|>0.6 (Part A threshold, no new hyperparameter choice).
- Output: `groups_168.json` with K groups (predicted K ≈ 30-50, depending on actual correlation structure).
- **Saved BEFORE training step**, immutable thereafter. Any subsequent grouping change requires re-running clustering + re-committing the artifact.

### 3.2 Step 2: Train Production Models on 168-dim

- Models: SAGE-Mean + MLP (Plan Z++ Part A standard)
- Seeds: 3 × Folds: 5 = 30 trained models per architecture, 60 total
- Hparams: locked Stage 0 defaults (lr=1e-3, wd=1e-4, hidden=64, num_layers=2, dropout=0.3, patience=10, epochs=50, optimizer=adam)
- Feature pipeline: 168-dim raw → per-fold winsor → per-fold scaler → model input
- Output: trained model objects retained in memory for inference step (not saved to disk, per Part A pattern)

### 3.3 Step 3: Grouped Permutation Δ-IC Inference

For each trained model and each group, replicate `compute_grouped_permutation_ic()` from [run_step3_plan_z_part_a.py:477-497](../run_step3_plan_z_part_a.py#L477):

```python
for grp in groups:
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed_for_group(seed, grp_label))
    for d in test_days:
        x = features_t[d].clone()
        perm = rng.permutation(num_stocks)
        x[:, grp_indices] = x[perm][:, grp_indices]   # CS shuffle just this group's features
        permuted_preds[d_idx] = model(x, full_ei)
    permuted_ic[grp] = daily_ic(permuted_preds, test_days, labels, label_valid)
```

Baseline IC (full features, no permutation) is also computed per model.

### 3.4 Step 4: Aggregate Δ-IC + Ranking

- Per (model, fold, seed) cell: `Δ-IC_grp = baseline_IC - permuted_IC_grp` (Part A definition)
- Per group: mean Δ-IC across 30 cells (2 models × 3 seeds × 5 folds) — or 60 if architecture grouped separately
- Rank groups by mean Δ-IC (descending: larger Δ = group more important)
- Statistical: per-group Δ-IC distribution + NW-HAC t-stat + bootstrap CI (replicates Part A statistical protocol)

### 3.5 Step 5: Hand-curated Mapping

- For each of 10 hand-curated features, find which group it belongs to in `groups_168.json`.
- Report that group's rank in extended universe + its mean Δ-IC.
- Output: `hand_curated_mapping_168.json`.

## §8. Open Questions for Codex Review

1. **Universe composition**: Should `hc_CORR5` be removed pre-clustering (since identical to Alpha158 `CORR5`) or kept (let clustering merge)?
   - Default: keep, let clustering merge.
2. **Clustering threshold**: |ρ|>0.6 was Plan Z++ Part A choice for 10 features. Is the same threshold appropriate for 168 features? Risk: too low produces too many groups (sparse Δ-IC estimates); too high merges semantically distinct features.
   - Default: |ρ|>0.6 (no change).
3. **Compute estimate (6-8h M4)**: realistic? Inference step (~10 min/model × 60 = 600 min = 10h) may be underestimated; should we trim seed count or fold count?
   - Default: 3 seeds × 5 folds × 2 models = 30 cells per architecture, matching Part A.
4. **Group ΔIC aggregation**: mean across 30 cells with NW-HAC SE? Or random-effects mixed model accounting for fold/seed clustering?
   - Default: mean + NW-HAC, matching Part A. RE model is potential upgrade.
5. **Baseline definition**: baseline IC computed once per (model, fold, seed) with all 168 features non-permuted; or computed afresh per group permutation (using non-permuted features for non-target groups)?
   - Default: once per (model, fold, seed). Matches Part A behavior in [run_step3_plan_z_part_a.py:477](../run_step3_plan_z_part_a.py#L477).
6. **Statistical test for "is group i significantly important"**: paired Wilcoxon? NW-HAC of ΔIC time series? BH-FDR across all K groups?
   - Default: NW-HAC t-stat on ΔIC time series + BH-FDR across groups (Part A protocol).
7. **Post-hoc framing in paper**: how to balance "this was preregistered before Plan Z++ Part B" (FALSE) vs "this used the exact same methodology" (TRUE)?
   - Default: label "post-hoc sensitivity analysis using preregistered method on extended universe; not preregistered universe".
8. **Failure mode**: if 60 trained models fail to converge on 168-dim (over-parameterization risk for SAGE-Mean), how to fall back?
   - Default: report training loss + early-stop epoch per model; if >20% don't converge, halt and reconsider.
9. **Comparison to Option B (LightGBM)**: should we report agreement/disagreement as a Spearman rank correlation between Plan AAA ranking and LightGBM gain ranking?
   - Default: yes, as supplementary table.
